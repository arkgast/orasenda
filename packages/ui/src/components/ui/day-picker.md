Seven toggle buttons for the days a routine runs, Monday first. At least one day stays on.

- **Anatomy:** a visible `label` (`body-sm`, `ink`) that names a `role="group"`, seven round day buttons in one row (they wrap only below 356px), and a helper line in `body-sm` `ink-muted` that describes the group (`aria-describedby`).
- **Props:** `label` (the group's name, "Repeat on"), `value` (the weekday mask: Monday = 1, Tuesday = 2, Wednesday = 4, Thursday = 8, Friday = 16, Saturday = 32, Sunday = 64; bits above 64 are ignored), `onChange(mask)`, `helper` (the host's line while two or more days are on, such as Rust's `Weekdays::label`: "Weekdays"), `disabled`, `className`. Slots: `day-picker-field`, `day-picker`, `day-picker-day` (with `data-day="monday"` … `"sunday"`), `day-picker-letter`, `day-picker-helper`.
- **Hit targets:** each day is a `hit-min` (44 × 44) circle with a `space-2` gap, so the row is 356px wide and fits a 360px dialog. Never shrink a day below `hit-min`.
- **States, by shape and weight as well as colour:** off is a hollow 2px `line-strong` ring with a regular `ink` letter on `surface-raised`; on is a filled `focus` disc with a bold `on-focus` letter (`aria-pressed="true"`). Hover darkens to `surface-sunken` (off) or `focus-hover` (on), except on the locked last day, which keeps its fill so it never looks pressable. Keyboard focus is the 2px `focus-ring` at a 2px offset. Disabled days keep their letter and take a dashed ring. In forced-colours mode an on day's ring grows to 4px, so it stays distinct when fills are dropped.
- **The last day on:** when exactly one day is on, that day keeps focus and its `aria-pressed="true"` but is `aria-disabled="true"` and does nothing on press. Its own `aria-describedby` points at the helper line, so VoiceOver reads the reason on the button itself, and the helper line reads "At least one day stays on." (after the host's `helper`, when there is one). Other days stay free to turn on. With no `helper` and two or more days on, the line reads "Pick the days it runs."
- **VoiceOver:** each button shows its short letter (`M T W T F S S`, `aria-hidden`) and is named by its full weekday ("Monday"), so the two T's and two S's are never ambiguous; the pressed state is read as "selected". The group is read with its label and helper line.
- **Keyboard:** Tab reaches each day; Space or Enter toggles it (native buttons). The days are not a radio group: any set of them can be on.
- **Copy:** English weekday names, in line with the rest of the app's copy.
