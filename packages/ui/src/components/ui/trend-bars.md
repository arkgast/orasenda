Vertical bars for a few periods side by side in Review Stats: 7 days, 8 weeks or 6 months. The vertical form of `BarRow`'s marks; a week reads left to right like the habit dot grid.

- **Anatomy:** a `figure` whose plot comes first and whose `figcaption` (the caption, `caption`, `ink-muted`) sits under it, `space-1` below the plot. The plot is 6, 7 or 8 columns at most `chart-column-width` (52px) wide, grouped at the start with `space-5` between them; in a narrow container they shrink, and a label wraps at spaces only (a single word may overhang into the gap, never into a neighbour's label). Each fill is `bar-height` wide with `radius-pill` ends, `line-strong`, rising from the baseline inside a plot `chart-height` (120px) tall. The top value reaches `chart-height` minus `space-2`, which leaves room for a stub above it, and a fill never leaves the plot. The label sits `space-1` under the plot in `caption`. No track, no axis, no gridlines. Fills scale to the larger of the top bar and the goal.
- **Current period:** its label is a word supplied by Rust ("Today", "This week", "This month") set in `font-semibold` and `ink`. Weight and the word carry it, never colour alone.
- **Empty period:** nothing recorded, or a future day: no fill, no stubs, only the label.
- **Goal (optional):** two `ink` stubs, each `1-5` (6px) long and 2px thick, `space-1` clear of the fill on each side, centred on every drawn column at the goal height and never across a fill. In narrow columns they may overhang into the gap. The goal words ("Goal 12h", `caption`, `ink`) appear once, after the last column, vertically centred on the goal height; they are not under the plot. A bar above the goal rises past the stubs. The taller of the top bar and the goal sets the top of the plot, so no bar leaves it.
- **VoiceOver:** the marks are `aria-hidden`. The figure is named by its caption and holds a visually hidden list, one item per period, each the `spoken` sentence ("Monday, 1 hour 5 minutes"). Nothing is focusable. No animation, so reduce-motion changes nothing.
- **Props:** `bars: { label, spoken, minutes, duration, current, empty }[]`, `goal?: { minutes, words }`, `caption`.
- **Contrast:** `line-strong` fill on `surface` 4.35 / 5.56 and on `surface-raised` 4.79 / 4.99 (3:1 needed for a mark); `ink` stubs and `ink-muted` labels pass 4.5:1 on both. In forced-colors the fill gets a border.

Do: let the words say the goal and the current period. Don't: show percentages, colour a bar by performance, add a red or amber, draw a bar for an empty period, or make a column focusable.
