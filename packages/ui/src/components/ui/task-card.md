A task on the board. Sessions beyond the estimate are information, never a warning.

- `title`, `type` (`deep` default or `shallow`), `project`, `frog` (the one hardest task of the day, shown as the word "Frog").
- `estimate` and `done` in sessions, `tracked` as a duration ("0h 41m"). The meta line reads "Est 3 · Done 1 · 0h 25m"; with no estimate it reads "No estimate". Dots cap at twelve, and are left out when there is no estimate and nothing done; sessions past the estimate are the same `focus` dot with a thin halo.
- `due` (worded by the host: "Due Friday"), `priority` (`high`, `medium`, `low`, shown as a word), `tags` (up to two shown) and `checklist` ("2 of 5") sit on the card. All of them are in the accessible name, so VoiceOver reads the same facts.
- In `density="card"` the title shows at most three lines, then an ellipsis. The full title stays in the accessible name (the button's label, or hidden text in the read-only form, where the visible title is `aria-hidden`).
- `onOpen` makes the card a native button. Without `onOpen` or `onSelect` the card is a read-only article with no tab stop.
- `onSelect` beside `onOpen`: a click selects (`selection` + "Selected"); Return or a double-click opens. Arrow keys come through `onKeyDown` (passed through untouched after the card's own Return rule), so the board moves the selection. `onSelect` alone makes a button that selects. Hover is `surface-sunken` only, never the selection ground.
- `density="row"` (TaskRow): a single line that wraps onto a second line, never letter by letter, when the column is too narrow for the type shape, state words and estimate together, at least `hit-min` tall, with the type shape, the title (ellipsis, kept whole in the name), the Frog and state words and the estimate in `timer-sm`. It drops the dots, tags and checklist. A read-only form (no button) serves the shutdown preview.
- `menuTabIndex` (`0 | -1`, default `0`): the More actions button's tab index. A card that roves passes `-1`, so the card is one tab stop (Tab leaves the board's columns); the button still opens by click.
- The menu key: on the focused card, `ContextMenu` or `Shift+F10` opens its menu with focus on the first item. The card calls `preventDefault` on the key and runs this before `onKeyDown`, and on the native `contextmenu` that follows a keyboard Shift+F10. With the menu open the key does nothing more. A menu opened by key returns focus to the card on Esc and on choosing; one opened by click returns it to the More button. A `disabled` card, a card with no `menu` and the read-only article ignore the key.
- `menu` adds a "More actions for <title>" icon button (tooltip `⇧F10`, `aria-keyshortcuts="Shift+F10"`) beside the card (never inside its button) that opens a `PopoverMenu`: "Move to <column>" for each of `moveTargets` (`onMove(id)`), "Make Frog" or "Unmark Frog" (`onFrog`), and "Remove" (`onRemove`). Items without a callback are left out. A `disabled` card shows no menu button. The trigger carries `aria-controls` while the menu is open. Repeated tags show once. It is the keyboard route to move a task; the board owns the pointer drag.
- `active` means a session is running on it: a `focus` bar and the words "In progress". `selected` is a separate state: `selection` ground and the word "Selected".
- **Short windows.** At `layout-compact-height` (480px) or less (inclusive, read from the token, so it follows the window height; 640x480 folds, the default 900x600 window never does) a card folds its session dots, due date and tags into ONE muted `body-sm` `ink-muted` line that truncates with an ellipsis, and the gap between lines goes from `space-3` to `space-2`. The type chip line, the title, the estimate line and the project are unchanged; the accessible name still carries everything, and the sheet shows the full detail. At 640x480 the full card keeps its 4px focus ring inside the column's scrollport. The row density is unchanged.

Do: let the column set the width. Don't: colour anything amber here, or show ratios of done to estimated.

- `moving`: the card is being dragged. It stays in place with a dashed `line-strong` edge, no shadow, never reduced opacity, and the word "Moving" (also in its accessible name). The word sits on the chip line (the top line never wraps: the state word sits right after the type chip and is never truncated; while moving or selected the priority is left off the line (it stays in the accessible name), and Frog then truncates if the line is still too narrow), so the card never grows when a drag starts; in a very narrow row it may still wrap. In card density "Selected" is also a word on the chip line, not its own line, and during a drag Moving replaces it (the selection ground is left off while moving, so the state is never colour alone); the card is as tall before, during and after a move (measured at 900x600 by `moving_card_height_steady_900x600` and `_640x480`). Do not add a border or outline offset: the 4px focus ring already reaches the scrollport's `p-1` edge.
