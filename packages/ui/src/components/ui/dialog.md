A confirmation. Escape always does the safe thing.

- `title` names the consequence ("Delete all tracking data?"). `description` is the body. `children` can add content.
- `confirmLabel` and `onConfirm`; leave `confirmLabel` out and there is no confirm button (the import sheet's source step). `confirmDisabledReason` disables the confirm and shows the reason under the actions, linked by `aria-describedby` ("Select at least one task to import."); `cancelLabel` (default "Cancel") and `onCancel`. Escape calls `onCancel`, and the cancel button takes initial focus.
- `destructive` styles the confirm as `critical` and adds the `scrim`; `scrim` alone is for a dialog that needs the backdrop without the destructive confirm. Other confirmations keep a clear backdrop. `loading` keeps the dialog open while the host works.
- `emphasis="safe"`: the cancel action is the primary and the initial focus, and Esc or Return choose it; the confirm is quiet. Used only for "End the session and the lock?" during Strong lock ("Keep going" / "End session"). For Gentle and Firm, End never asks.
- Radix Dialog (`role="dialog"`), or AlertDialog (`role="alertdialog"`) when `destructive`: background inert, focus contained, focus restored to whatever had it when the dialog opened (the trigger). Backdrop clicks never dismiss. Esc closes only the topmost layer (`src/lib/layer.ts`).
- Sizes: the panel is capped at `panel-width` and shrinks to the window with a `space-3` gutter; padding and gaps are token steps.
- `size="sheet"` is the task sheet: capped at `sheet-width` (720px), always over the `scrim`, and `children` sit in a two-column field grid that stacks to one column when the sheet body is narrower than `container-sheet-stack` (600px). A field that needs the full width spans both columns (`@sheet-stack:col-span-2`). Edits in a sheet save on change, so its confirm reads "Done" and its cancel "Close"; a sheet that stages one action (the import) has that action as its confirm, named for what it does ("Import 12 tasks"), with "Cancel", and `loading` shows its progress ("Importing…"); Esc closes it and focus returns to the opener. In a sheet the title and the actions row stay pinned and only the field body scrolls when the window is short, so Done is always visible; the body keeps `space-3` of scroll padding so a focused field stays clear of the edge. When focus moves into the body (Tab included), the body scrolls at once, never smoothly, until the focused field and its focus ring sit inside that padding, because WebKit alone would stop at the field's own box and clip the ring. Pointer focus (a click) is left alone so the field never moves under the pointer: a focus on an element that contains the pressed target, or on the control of a pressed label, counts as pointer focus, so a press WebKit does not focus (Remove item, Remove tag) still lets the code-moved focus nudge. The press ends a frame after pointerup, on a key press, or on the next frame. A field taller than the body is aligned by its top so the top of its ring stays visible. A panel scrolls as a whole.
- Plain Tab wraps through a sheet's text fields. With macOS keyboard navigation off, WebKit's Tab visits only text fields, and from the last one the focus trap would hold focus there. A plain Tab (no Alt) from the last text field goes to the first, and Shift+Tab from the first goes to the last, as native cycling does. Forward, it acts only when focus did not move on its own, so with full keyboard access Tab still goes on to Close and Done. Once a plain Tab has landed on a non-text control in the body (full keyboard access is on), Shift+Tab keeps the browser's order too. Known gap: Alt+Tab does not wrap (a follow-up covers it), and its path out of a roving checklist row is tracked separately.
- `initialFocusRef` (optional) names the element that takes focus when the dialog opens instead of the cancel action. The task sheet passes its title field, because editing is its purpose; Esc and Close still return focus to the opener.

Do: mount one dialog at a time and keep the trigger mounted. Don't: perform the deletion in the component; the host does, after `onConfirm`.
