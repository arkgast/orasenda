A board column: a `surface-sunken` well with a heading, a count and the cards.

- `title`, `count` (actual tasks), `limit` (the WIP policy the user chose). Under the limit the count is a plain caption ("2 of 3"); at the limit it is the plain caption "At limit" (a Doing limit of 1 is the normal working state and must not read amber all day). Only over the limit does the `warning` chip appear, reading "4 of 3" with its shape. Never a red badge. With no limit the caption reads "N tasks" ("1 task"). A folded strip over its limit shows the alert shape above the count, and its name reads "Today, 4 of 3".
- `width="wide"` grows to `column-wide-width` (Today and Doing in the Today-first board); the default grows to `column-width`. A wide column starts from `column-width` and grows twice as fast as a standard one, so at the default window Today and Doing are visibly wider than Backlog. No open column is ever narrower than `column-min-width`; nothing folds for room (the board squeezes columns to that width, then scrolls sideways).
- `collapsed` (which requires `onToggle`) folds the column into a `column-strip-width` strip (`surface-sunken`, `radius-lg`) with a chevron, the name and the count, a native button with `aria-expanded="false"` named "Done, 5 tasks". Hover: the chevron and count turn `ink` and an inset 1px `line-strong` edge appears; pressed: `selection`. An open column with `onToggle` shows a "Fold <title>" icon button (`aria-expanded="true"`) in the top-right of its header.
- **Header, two rows** (`kanban-column-header.tsx`): row one is the name (wrapping) with the Fold button top-right; row two is the count caption (or the chip) at the start and + at the end. Every button is `hit-min`. At `column-min-width` row two holds the "4 of 3" chip and the 44px + without overlap.
- `onAdd` adds the + `IconButton` (the `plus` glyph), named "Add task to <title>" or `addLabel`; without `onAdd` there is no +.
- `onEdit` makes the name a quiet button inside the `h2` (reads as the heading at rest; an inset 1px `line-strong` edge on hover; the focus ring on focus). A double-click calls `onEdit`; a single pointer click does nothing. Return, Space and VoiceOver's activate (a click with `detail === 0`) call it. Its description is "Double-click or press Return to edit the column.", a hidden sibling of the `h2` so it names neither the heading nor the region. Without `onEdit` the name is plain heading text.
- `editor` replaces both header rows, Fold included; the cards stay and the region is named by `aria-label`. The editor sits in a `kanban-column-editor` slot that shrinks (`min-h-0`) and scrolls (`overflow-y-auto`) inside the column, with `p-1` and `scroll-py-1` so focus rings are not clipped; in a short window Save and Cancel stay reachable and Tab to a clipped control scrolls this slot, not the board.
- Data slots for the board's arrow roving: `kanban-column-name` (the button), and `kanban-column-add` and `kanban-column-fold` (a `display: contents` wrapper around the button; the board finds the `button` inside).
- `id` lands on the region, or on the strip when folded, so the board can rove focus between columns.
- `footer` renders under the cards, inside the column (an "Add task" control). The cards scroll inside the column (`overflow-y-auto`); the column, not the board, owns the scroll. The cards area keeps `scroll-py-1` (the ring's reach), so a card scrolled to the edge keeps its whole focus ring.
- Fold rules: nothing folds for room; folds are manual (the Fold button, or a click, Return or Space on the strip). Done starts folded, and the host saves folds. After Fold, focus moves to the strip that replaces the column; after opening, the host moves it.
- `children` are `TaskCard`s (`density="row"` in a narrow Backlog); `emptyMessage` defaults to "Nothing here yet. That's fine."

Do: keep adding tasks possible past the limit; the limit informs, it does not block. Don't: implement drag here; the board owns the drag and the column only draws the slot. The card menu and the editor provide keyboard move.

- `drop` (`{ index, label }`): a drag is over this column. A `hit-min` slot with a dashed `line-strong` edge (`radius-md`, never shrinks) reads `label` ("Moving <title> here") in `ink`, `body-sm`, inserted before the child at `index` in `children` exactly as passed (an index at or past the length appends; an empty column shows the slot instead of the empty message). The column does no counting of its own: no `TaskCard` checks and no `moving` filtering. The board computes the index, accounting for a leading new-task field and for leaving the dragged card out. The slot is `aria-hidden`: the board's status line says it aloud. On a folded strip `index` is ignored: the strip takes the `selection` ground with an inset `line-strong` edge and the words "Move here" (also in its name).
- The drop slot opens over `duration-calm` with `ease-calm` (its height grows from one text line to `hit-min`), and appears at once under reduce-motion (`motion-reduce:transition-none`).
