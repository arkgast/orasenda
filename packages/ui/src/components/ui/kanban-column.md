A board column: a `surface-sunken` well with a heading, a count and the cards.

- `title`, `count` (actual tasks), `limit` (the WIP policy the user chose). Under the limit the count is a plain caption ("2 of 3"); at the limit it is the plain caption "At limit" (a Doing limit of 1 is the normal working state and must not read amber all day). Only over the limit does the `warning` chip appear, reading "4 of 3" with its shape. Never a red badge. With no limit the caption reads "N tasks" ("1 task"). A folded strip over its limit shows the alert shape above the count, and its name reads "Today, 4 of 3".
- `width="wide"` grows to `column-wide-width` (Today and Doing in the Today-first board); the default grows to `column-width`. A wide column starts from `column-width` and grows twice as fast as a standard one, so at the default window Today and Doing are visibly wider than Backlog. No open column is ever narrower than `column-min-width` (the board folds or uses the compact picker instead of squeezing) and the board never scrolls sideways.
- `collapsed` (which requires `onToggle`) folds the column into a `column-strip-width` strip (`surface-sunken`, `radius-lg`) with a chevron, the name and the count, a native button with `aria-expanded="false"` named "Done, 5 tasks". Hover: the chevron and count turn `ink` and an inset 1px `line-strong` edge appears; pressed: `selection`. An open column with `onToggle` shows a "Fold <title>" icon button (`aria-expanded="true"`) in its header.
- `action` is the header's action slot (a column menu). `id` lands on the region, or on the strip when folded, so the board can rove focus between columns.
- `footer` renders under the cards, inside the column (an "Add task" control). The cards scroll inside the column (`overflow-y-auto`); the column, not the board, owns the scroll.
- Fold rules (the board decides, the column only folds): opening a strip folds the least recently opened column, never the one holding the running task, and moves focus to the opened column's first card (or its heading when empty). Columns fold in one order everywhere, first to last: Backlog, Doing, Today (Today stays open longest); Done starts folded. After Fold, focus moves to the strip that replaces the column; after opening, the host moves it (above).
- `children` are `TaskCard`s (`density="row"` in a narrow Backlog); `emptyMessage` defaults to "Nothing here yet. That's fine."
