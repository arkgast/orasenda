The Focus window (`full-screen-focus.tsx`): the time, one quiet way out, and the controls for the session, nothing else.

- **Anatomy:** four fixed tracks and one flexible time track, top to bottom: the top row (the host's `summary`, centred, while idle or ended; Leave full screen, an `IconButton` with the Esc `Kbd`, at the end), the status line, the time track, and the lower region that holds the controls row and the hint row. Timer surfaces never scroll.
- **Props:** `TimerRing` props (`remaining`, `remainingLabel`, `progress`, `mode`, `clock`) plus `onLeave`, `hint`, `onPause`, `onResume`, `onEnd`, `onStart`, `summary`, `singleKeys`, `ended`, `status`, `secondaryActions`, `phase` and `offer`.
- **Layout:** it fills its host (the Focus window's body, which the host sizes to the window) and measures its own box with a `ResizeObserver`. In that callback, and nowhere else, it calls `timerLayout` with the box from TimerStrip's README: `contentWidth` and `contentHeight` are its width and height minus `space-8` (minus `space-4` below `layout-compact-width`), `windowWidth` is its width. A tick never re-runs it. `ring` shows `TimerRing size="fluid"` with `clock`; `strip` and `stackedStrip` show `TimerStrip` (`stacked` for the second) and no clock. The section carries the result as `data-layout`.
- **Margins and gaps:** the ring from `layout-compact-width` wide: `space-6` on each side (the `space-8` of the box) and `space-4` between tracks. Below `layout-compact-width`, and in both strips: `space-2` on each side and `space-1` between tracks, so short windows keep their time track.
- **Lower region:** in the ring from `layout-compact-width`, the time track stops at `timer-full-size` and the lower region takes all the remaining height (at least 116: `control-lg` plus `space-6` twice). The controls row is centred in it, so it sits midway between the ring's bottom and the view's bottom at every size; the hint row is pinned `space-2` from the bottom and never moves the controls. Below `layout-compact-width` and in the strips the region is the controls row and the hint row, `space-1` apart, at the bottom of the box (the same stack as before: little height to spare, so Pause stays near the time).
- **Fixed tracks:** top `hit-min` (44), status 18 (one `body-sm` line, reserved even when empty, so nothing moves when a session ends), and the lower region: at least 116 in the ring (52 plus `space-6` twice), growing with the view; in the compact ring and the strips the controls (52) and the hint row (18) stack. In strips, the digits follow the time track's height as well as its width (never below `timer-md`).

## The cases the harness checks

- 1280 × 720 (ring, 460px track), 1920 × 1080 (ring 560), the phone 390 × 844 and xl 1440 × 900 (both ring), the strip frame 1000 × 440 (strip, 276px track) and the minimum 360 × 240 (stacked strip), each in focus, paused, idle, break, warning and ended.
- At the minimum window (360 × 240) the box is 344 × 224 and the result is `stackedStrip`. The fixed tracks and gaps take 148, which leaves a 76px time track for the stacked strip's head (the chip and the digits, about 30px here), `space-1` and the 10px bar.

## Controls and keys

- **By mode:** during a session, Pause (`round` `IconButton`, "Pause session"), or Resume ("Resume session") while paused, with End ("End session", ⌘.) beside it, centred, `space-8` between the two in the ring (the narrower gaps below apply in the compact layouts). Idle or `ended`: Start, the `StartButton`'s primary with the play glyph, named "Start focus" (there is no Just 2 minutes here). Each calls its handler; the host changes the mode.
- **Secondary actions:** `secondaryActions?: ReadonlyArray<{ id, label, name?, onSelect }>`, zero to two, rendered after End in the controls row as `md` `Button`s that the host drives ("+10 min", "Skip break", "Take a break", "Keep going", "Stop here"). Only the first two render; a third is dropped without a sign, so the host passes at most two. One action is `quiet`; two (the keep-going offer, and a break with "+10 min" then "Skip break") are `quiet` then `ghost`, except during the offer (below). `name` is the accessible name when it says more than the label ("Add 10 minutes of focus", "Keep going with Steady Focus"); the visible label is then `aria-hidden`. Pause or Resume stays the only round primary. The controls track stays `control-lg` (52): the gap narrows from `space-6` to `space-4` with one action and `space-2` with two. With two actions outside the offer, below `layout-compact-width` and in the strips, the gap narrows further to `space-1-5` so End, "+10 min" and "Skip break" fit 344px; the actions keep their padding. During the offer (`offer` true while idle, set by the host; never inferred from the count) the first action is the screen's one primary (`lg`, as Start is) and the second is `ghost`, and they stand in for Start, because Start, "Keep going" and "Stop here" together do not fit 344px at the window minimum. Nothing in the component decides when an action shows; the host passes none by default.
- **Break phase:** `phase?: "focus" | "break"` is set by the host, because a break's last 2:00 arrives as `mode="warning"` and a paused break as `mode="paused"`. It defaults to "break" when `mode` is "break", else "focus". In a break, End is named "End break" and the round button "Pause break" or "Resume break" (same ⌘. shortcut, same handlers).
- **Leaving:** the Leave `IconButton` and Esc call `onLeave` at once. There is no confirmation `Dialog`; the host decides what leaving does. Esc inside the host's open `PopoverMenu` closes only the menu.
- **Hint:** `hint?: { text, visible }` is the key hint ("Space pauses · Esc leaves") in the hint row. While `visible` it shows; when `visible` turns false it fades over `duration-slow`, and the row keeps its 18px height (`opacity` only, never `display`), so nothing moves. Faded, it is `aria-hidden`. Under reduce motion it disappears with no fade. The host owns the timing: it shows the hint for `duration-hint` (4 s) on the first three entries and then sets `visible` false. Without `hint`, the row shows the static caption below.
- **Esc while the Leave tooltip shows:** the section carries `data-os-esc`. An Esc pressed inside it while a tooltip shows (Leave's, on key focus or under the pointer) hides that tooltip and still reaches the section, so one press leaves. In the Focus window Esc has one job, leaving; a press that only hid a tooltip would read as Esc not working (Tooltip README, Keyboard). An Esc inside the host's open `PopoverMenu` closes only the menu.
- **Caption (`singleKeys`, without `hint`):** true: "Space pauses · Esc leaves" ("Space resumes" while paused, "Space starts" while idle or ended), and Pause's tooltip shows Space. Omitted or false: "⌘. ends · Esc leaves" ("⌘↩ starts · Esc leaves" while idle or ended). Start's `aria-keyshortcuts` follows it: `Space Meta+Enter` or `Meta+Enter`. Each key is a `Kbd` with its spoken name. Space, ⌘. and ⌘↩ are the host's keys; the component handles only Esc.
- **Focus:** the section ("Full-screen focus") takes focus on mount, so Esc works at once. Tab order: summary (idle or ended) → Leave full screen → Pause or Resume, or Start → End → the secondary actions, in order.

## States and announcements

- **`ended`:** the status line says "Session ended.", the timer shows idle, Start and the summary return, and the window stays open.
- **Live region:** one, the status line: an always-mounted `role="status"` paragraph that holds "Session ended." while `ended` and otherwise only an `aria-hidden` non-breaking space (it keeps the line's height), so the end is announced once. The host may pass `status` (a failed command, an unreadable timer): that one line takes the place of "Session ended." in the same region, and nothing else changes. The status stays on one line (it truncates with an ellipsis rather than wrapping, so the time track never loses height); the live region still reads the full text. The Focus window is its own window and has no page status to announce through. The timer stays `role="timer"`, `aria-live="off"`.
- It is a borderless window per display, not macOS native full screen.
