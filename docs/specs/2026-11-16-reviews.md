## TL;DR
- The rail's **Review** placeholder (⌘6) becomes a real page with three parts: **Today's shutdown**, **This week's review and plan**, and **Stats** over weeks and months.
- **Daily shutdown** (2–5 minutes, four steps): unfinished tasks (choose which go back to Backlog), a read-only look at tomorrow, three optional reflection prompts, then "Shutdown complete."
- **Weekly review and plan** (four steps): a look back at the week in words and small bars, one improvement and notes, up to three optional priorities picked from the Board or typed new, then "Week planned."
- **Stats** are words first, with calm shapes: focus-time bars (with the person's weekly goal as a quiet line), routine and habit counts, existing dot grids, and estimated vs actual. A **buffer suggestion** (#585) appears only after enough history and is applied only when the person accepts it.
- Reflections and priorities live in one new syncable table, `reflection` (migration 0010). Both nudges are off by default.

## Change in a nutshell
Friday, 17:30. The person presses ⌘6.

```
 Review                                                   [ Start weekly review ]
 Today · Friday 20 November
   Ready when you are.                                    ( Start shutdown )
 This week · planning the week of 23 November
   Not planned yet.
 Stats                                [ Week | 8 weeks | 6 months ]
   This week: 9h 40m of focus in 14 sessions. Goal 12h.
   ▁ ▃ ▅ ▂ ▆ · ·     Mon Tue Wed Thu Fri Sat Sun
   Morning Light & Movement: finished 4 times.
   Drink a glass of water: done on 5 of 5 scheduled days.  ●●●●●○○ …
```

1. *Start weekly review* (the primary, because it is Friday and next week has no plan). Step 1 of 4 shows the week in sentences, day bars, focus time per task, and the five shutdown reflections written this week, read back without scores. *Continue*.
2. Step 2: "One improvement for next week" and "Notes", both optional. *Continue*.
3. Step 3: "Priorities for next week (up to 3, optional)". Typing "sync" lists *Fix sync bug · Doing*; Return picks it. Typing "Plan the offsite" and choosing *Add "Plan the offsite" to Backlog* adds a new task. *Continue*.
4. "Week planned." with *Back to Review* focused. In the Board's TaskPicker, both tasks now read "· This week".

Later that evening, *Start shutdown*: two unfinished tasks in Today, one set to *Back to Backlog*; tomorrow says "Saturday 21 November. No routines scheduled. Nothing due."; the three prompts are left empty; "Shutdown complete."

## Problem
P4 promises reviews, and its exit criterion is a week of use with reviews completed. Today the Review page is a placeholder (`apps/orasenda/src/app/App.tsx:327-338`), the Evening Shutdown template is a plain checklist (`docs/specs/2026-11-14-routines-and-habits.md:138`), and nothing shows the record over more than 28 days. The Board records estimated vs actual sessions, but the buffer is a fixed setting (`docs/specs/2026-11-14-board.md:104-105`, `:206`). The ADHD audience needs a closing ritual that takes minutes, a plan that can be skipped, and stats that never read as a score.

## Goals / Success criteria
1. From the Review page or ⌘K, a person can complete the shutdown and the weekly review by keyboard alone and with VoiceOver, and can finish either with every optional part left empty.
2. A completed shutdown writes one `reflection` row for its shutdown day; a completed weekly review writes one row for its planned week, with 0 to 3 priorities. Saving again updates the same row.
3. A typed priority adds one task at the end of Backlog in the same write as the review.
4. Stats show focus time per day (Week), per week (8 weeks) and per month (6 months), routine finishes and habit counts on scheduled days. Every number is also in a sentence worded by Rust, and nothing uses a percentage (except the buffer's own setting value), a ranking or a comparison with an earlier period.
5. The buffer suggestion appears only under the threshold in [Buffer suggestion](#buffer-suggestion) and changes `board.estimate_buffer` only when the person presses *Use NN%*.
6. Both nudges are off by default and post only when their preference is on and the status is `granted`, at most once per day each.
7. Reflections survive export, merge import and backups, and Delete all data removes them.
8. No copy uses "missed", "failed", "broken" or "streak lost", an exclamation mark or an emoji (a copy test greps the Rust word modules and the feature).
9. Every new or changed component has its README, a component test and a specimen in both themes; `just contrast` passes; `./scripts/ci.sh` is green and every file stays at 500 lines or fewer.

## Non-goals
- Calendar blocks: SPEC 2.5's "plan next week's deep blocks", tomorrow's events and the realistic finish time (P3).
- Distraction data: "top distractions", bypass reasons, the focus vs distracting split (P5).
- Time per project or tag (follow-up). Leaderboards, sharing or any comparison with other people (never).
- A big streak number or a score of any kind (routines Decision 6).
- Editing or deleting one reflection from a list, and a reflection history page (follow-up).
- A choice of week start or of shutdown weekdays (follow-ups).

## Users & permissions
One local person, no accounts. The nudges use the existing notification permission and ask for nothing new. Reflections are personal writing: syncable like every other user entity (ADR-002), never sent anywhere until P10 sync, and covered by Delete all data.

## Tenancy & localization
- English copy, plain and kind. Rust words every date, duration, count and sentence (ADR-011); the webview assembles none.
- **Days** are local dates from `LocalDate::from_epoch_ms(ms, offset)` (`crates/core/src/routines/date.rs:92`), with the current offset, as the routines do. A focus session counts toward the local date it started on.
- **Weeks start on Monday**, like the dot grid (routines Decision 5). Months are calendar months.
- **Shutdown day.** A shutdown started before 04:00 local belongs to the previous date, so a 00:40 shutdown closes the day that just ended and "tomorrow" is the date it now is. The shutdown day is frozen when the flow starts. *Lean (owner may overrule on the canvas).* Everything else (stats, routines, the Board's Frog) keeps local midnight.
- **Planned week.** Monday to Thursday, the weekly review plans the current week and looks back on the week before. Friday to Sunday, it plans next week and looks back on the current week so far. *Lean (owner may overrule on the canvas).*

## Domain model
New CONTEXT.md terms: Shutdown, Shutdown day, Reflection, Weekly review, Planned week, Look-back, Weekly priority, Focus time, Stats range, Time away, Buffer suggestion.

### `reflection` (syncable, `change_log` like `inbox_note`)
| Field | Type | Rules |
|---|---|---|
| `id` | TEXT PK | `shutdown:<YYYY-MM-DD>` or `week:<YYYY-MM-DD>` (the planned week's Monday), so two devices agree, as `habit_check` does |
| `kind` | TEXT | `shutdown` or `week` |
| `period` | TEXT | the shutdown day, or the planned week's Monday; `GLOB` CHECK like `habit_check.day` |
| `went_well`, `to_improve`, `outside_control` | TEXT NULL | shutdown only; trimmed, ≤ 1 000 characters, empty is NULL |
| `improvement` | TEXT NULL | week only; ≤ 200 |
| `notes` | TEXT NULL | week only; ≤ 2 000 |
| `priorities` | TEXT NOT NULL DEFAULT `'[]'` | week only; JSON array of 0–3 distinct task ids, in order |
| `completed_at` | INTEGER NULL | set when the flow reaches its last step; kept on later saves |
| `created_at`, `updated_at` | INTEGER | |
| `deleted_at` | INTEGER NULL | tombstone; nothing in this spec writes one, but merge import and sync honour it |

CHECKs tie the shutdown fields to `kind = 'shutdown'` and the week fields to `kind = 'week'`. `Entity` gains `Reflection`.

### Rules
- **A reflection is never scored.** It is shown back as the person's own words, with its day, and never counted ("5 reflections") or summarised.
- **A weekly priority** is a live task id. A priority whose task was removed is dropped from every view (the row keeps the id; Undo on the Board brings it back). A Done task reads "Done" with a check shape.
- **Focus time** is the sum of `focused_ms` over phase `focus` sessions, quick and ended early included: the same total as the deep-hours cap (CONTEXT, *Deep-hours cap*). The UI says "focus", never "deep hours", because Deep is also a task kind.
- **Time away**: a run of 14 or more local days with nothing recorded for the thing being counted (no focus session, or no check-in of that habit on a scheduled day). Counts that have a denominator (habit days) start the day after the latest time away and say "Since <date>". Totals (focus time, routine finishes) are never affected, because they have no denominator. No sentence names, counts or recaps the gap (design-system README, *One-time tips*: "A returning user is never welcomed back, counted or recapped").
- **Evening Shutdown routines stay plain routines.** Nothing reads `routine.template` to change behaviour (routines Decision 7, `docs/specs/2026-11-14-routines-and-habits.md:181`). The template data does not change, and a completed shutdown checks in no habit. See [FAQ](#how-do-evening-shutdown-routines-relate-to-the-guided-shutdown).

### Buffer suggestion
- **Eligible task:** Done, `done_at` in the last 56 local days, an estimate, and at least one linked focus session (`tasks::tracked`, `crates/store/src/repo/tasks.rs:299`). Actual is the Board's session count, so the numbers match the card's "Actual 4 sessions".
- **Threshold:** at least 10 eligible tasks, done in at least 3 different weeks. *Lean (owner may overrule on the canvas)*: ten tasks keep one long outlier from swinging a ratio of sums, and three weeks is "a few weeks of linked sessions" (#585). It cannot appear in the first week of use, which is honest.
- **Value:** `ratio = Σ actual / Σ estimate`; `suggested = clamp(round_to_5((ratio − 1) × 100), 25, 50)`, in integers in core, ties rounding up.
- **Shown** only when `suggested` differs from the current buffer and from the value last set aside with *Not now* (device-only `meta` key `hint.buffer_suggestion`).

### Nudges (`crates/service/src/review/nudge.rs`)
Called from the scheduler tick beside `routines::nudge_due` (`crates/service/src/scheduler/tick.rs:44`) and built like it (`crates/service/src/routines/nudge.rs:46`): core `due_now` decides the 30-minute window (`crates/core/src/routines/due.rs:11`).
- Shutdown: Monday to Friday at `review.shutdown_time`, never once today's shutdown is complete. "Ready to close the day when you are." *Lean (owner may overrule on the canvas)*: weekdays only, because a work shutdown on a weekend nudges toward work.
- Weekly: on `review.weekly_day` at the shutdown time, never once the planned week is complete. "Your weekly review is ready when you are." If both are due in the same minute, only the weekly one posts and the shutdown counts as nudged.
- Same gate as the routines: the preference on **and** the status `granted`, read only when due; an in-memory `(kind, date)` set.

### Review page (`apps/orasenda/src/features/review/`)

Section **Today** (day label, `line`, *Look again* after completion). Section **This week** (label, `line`, the priorities as a plain list with "Done" in words). Section **Stats**: a `Choice` (Week, 8 weeks, 6 months; default Week), the summary sentences, `TrendBars`, the routine and habit lines with each habit's `HabitDots` (`columns="week"`, legend once), the estimate line, and the buffer suggestion as an `InlineNotice` with *Use 40%* (secondary) and *Not now* (quiet).

## Behavior & edge cases
- **Saving.** Each *Continue* writes (`shutdown_save` or `weekly_save`); the last step calls `*_complete`. *Look again* reopens the flow with saved answers; completing again keeps the first `completed_at`. Only the current step's unsaved typing is lost on reload, because the flow's position is webview state (Review is main-window only).
- **A shutdown across midnight** keeps the day frozen at its start. A shutdown on a day with no focus and no tasks is normal: every step still works.
- **Priorities.** A fourth pick is refused with `Full` (the UI never offers it). The same task twice is ignored. A new title is 1–200 characters (Board rules) and lands at the end of Backlog in the review's transaction; Undo of the review is not offered, the task is removed on the Board like any other.
- **Moving tasks.** Undo restores each task's lane and rank. If the person edited a task in between, Undo still restores the lane; a removed task stays removed.
- **Empty states.** No data at all: "Nothing recorded yet. Stats appear after your first focus session." A range with no sessions: the bars are all empty stubs and the summary says "No focus recorded in these 8 weeks." No reflections this week: the look-back leaves that part out.
- **Time away.** Example: a habit with check-ins until 1 October, nothing until 3 November, then 9 of 10 days: "Since 3 November: done on 9 of 10 scheduled days." The 8-week bars still show the empty weeks as stubs; no line mentions them.
- **Copy.** Routine lines count finishes only ("Wind-down: finished 6 times."), never runs against days. No "missed", "failed", "broken" or "streak lost"; no percentages except the buffer's own setting value; a reached goal is said plainly. `amber` and `critical` are not used anywhere here.
- **Store unavailable.** "Orasenda can't load your review right now." + Try again + "It's kept on this Mac." A failed save rolls back to Rust's last view and says "Orasenda can't save your reflection right now." (design-system README, *Copy patterns*).
- **Import and sync.** A merged reflection for today replaces the open flow's answers only on the next step; `review-changed` follows every import that touched the table.
- **Reduce motion.** Step changes cross-fade with `duration-calm`; under reduce-motion they change at once. Bars never animate.
- **Accessibility.** Hit targets `hit-min`; the TaskPicker and toggles follow their READMEs; each step's heading is the focus target; bars are read as a list of sentences.

## Testing strategy
Tests first for every unit.
- **Core:** `week_start` (Sunday, Monday, year end), `planned_week` (each weekday), `shutdown_day` (03:59 and 04:00, DST offsets), `focus_buckets` (session on a month edge, empty periods, current flag), `scheduled_count` and `after_time_away` (13 vs 14 days, rest days ignored, created mid-range), `suggest` (9 tasks or 2 weeks give `None`, rounding to 5, clamps at 25 and 50, ratio below 1).
- **Service words:** table tests for every sentence (durations, "Since …", goal reached and not, no goal, the estimate line, the buffer line, empty ranges) and a test that greps the word modules for the banned words and "!".
- **Store:** migration 9 → 10 on a filled database; `reflections::upsert` change-log rows (only changed fields); CHECKs refuse week fields on a shutdown row; `routine_runs::between`; `move_to_backlog_end` and `restore_lanes`; merge import of `reflection`.
- **Service:** every command through the recording host, including `Full`, `Empty`, `TooLong`, a removed priority, the new-title task in the same transaction, `primary` on each weekday, emit after unlock, the midnight emit, and both nudges (off, on, denied, already complete, the same-minute rule).
- **App (vitest):** `ReviewPage` tests split by behaviour (overview and primary, stats ranges, buffer suggestion use and *Not now*, failure lines), `ShutdownFlow` and `WeeklyFlow` by keyboard (Tab, Return, Esc, focus after each step, live region), the palette asks, and the Settings rows. ## FAQ
### How do Evening Shutdown routines relate to the guided shutdown?
They are separate. A template is copied, never linked, so a copy is the person's routine and the app does not change what it does. A person can keep the routine as a checklist, remove it, or use the Review page instead. Changing the template's steps to point at Review would only affect new copies; that is left to the owner (Open questions).

### What do "move unfinished tasks" and "preview tomorrow" mean before P3?
There is no calendar yet, so nothing is scheduled. Moving means choosing which Today and Doing tasks go back to Backlog; the rest stay where they are. The preview reads what Orasenda already knows about tomorrow (routines, due dates, the Today column and its buffer, the week's priorities) and invents nothing. P3 adds events and blocks.

### Why one shared table and not `shutdown` and `weekly_review` tables?
Both are small, personal writing keyed by a period, and both travel through the same portability and sync rules. One repository, one merge rule, and SPEC 4's single `Review` entity.

### Why no "up from last week"?
A comparison turns a record into a score, and for a person returning from time away it is a recap of the gap. Values and the person's own goal are enough.

### Why is the suggestion never applied by itself?
The buffer is the person's planning choice (Board Decision 7). History can inform it; only the person changes it (calm optional controls rule).
