## TL;DR
- The rail's **Review** placeholder (⌘6) becomes a real page with three parts: **Today's shutdown**, **This week's review and plan**, and **Stats** over weeks and months.
- **Daily shutdown** (2–5 minutes, four steps): unfinished tasks (choose which go back to Backlog), a read-only look at tomorrow, three optional reflection prompts, then "Shutdown complete."
- **Weekly review and plan** (four steps): a look back at the week in words and small bars, one improvement and notes, up to three optional priorities picked from the Board or typed new, then "Week planned."
- **Stats** are words first, with calm shapes: focus-time bars (with the person's weekly goal as a quiet tick), routine and habit counts, existing dot grids, and estimated vs actual. A **buffer suggestion** (#585) appears only after enough history and is applied only when the person accepts it.
- Reflections and priorities live in one new syncable table, `reflection` (migration 0010). Both nudges are off by default.

## Change in a nutshell
Friday, 17:30. The person presses ⌘6.

```
 Review                                                   [ Start weekly review ]
 Today · Friday 20 November
   Ready when you are.                                    ( Start shutdown )
 This week · planning the week of 23 November
   Not planned yet.
 Stats                                [ Week | 8 weeks | 6 months ]
   This week: 9h 40m of focus in 14 sessions. Goal 12h.
   ▁ ▃ ▅ ▂ ▆ · ·     Mon Tue Wed Thu Fri Sat Sun
   Morning Light & Movement: finished 4 times.
   Drink a glass of water: done on 5 days this week.  ●●●●●○○ …
```

1. *Start weekly review* (the primary, because it is Friday and next week has no plan). Step 1 of 4 shows the week in sentences, day bars, focus time per task, and the five shutdown reflections written this week, read back without scores. *Continue*.
2. Step 2: "One improvement for next week" and "Notes", both optional. *Continue*.
3. Step 3: "Priorities for next week (up to 3, optional)". Typing "sync" lists *Fix sync bug · Doing*; Return picks it. Typing "Plan the offsite" and choosing *Add "Plan the offsite" to Backlog* adds a new task. *Continue* saves the review.
4. "Week planned." with *Back to Review* focused. From Monday 23 November, both tasks read "· This week" in the TaskPicker.

Later that evening, *Start shutdown*: two unfinished tasks in Today, one set to *Back to Backlog*; tomorrow says "Saturday 21 November. No routines scheduled. Nothing due."; the three prompts are left empty; "Shutdown complete."

## Problem
P4 promises reviews, and its exit criterion is a week of use with reviews completed. Today the Review page is a placeholder (`apps/orasenda/src/app/App.tsx:328-339`), the Evening Shutdown template is a plain checklist (`docs/specs/2026-11-14-routines-and-habits.md:138`), and nothing shows the record over more than 28 days. The Board records estimated vs actual sessions, but the buffer is a fixed setting (`docs/specs/2026-11-14-board.md:104-105`, `:206`). The ADHD audience needs a closing ritual that takes minutes, a plan that can be skipped, and stats that never read as a score.

## Goals / Success criteria
1. From the Review page or ⌘K, a person can complete the shutdown and the weekly review by keyboard alone and with VoiceOver, and can finish either with every optional part left empty.
2. A completed shutdown writes one `reflection` row for its shutdown day; a completed weekly review writes one row for its planned week, with 0 to 3 priorities. Saving again updates the same row. A write names its period, and Rust refuses any period but the current or the previous one.
3. A typed priority adds exactly one task at the end of Backlog, in the same write as the review, however often the review is saved.
4. Stats show focus time per day (Week), per week (8 weeks) and per month (6 months), routine finishes and habit days done. Every number is also in a sentence worded by Rust, and nothing uses a percentage (except the buffer's own setting value), a ranking or a comparison with an earlier period.
5. The buffer suggestion appears only under the threshold in [Buffer suggestion](#buffer-suggestion) and changes `board.estimate_buffer` only when the person presses *Use NN%*.
6. Both nudges are off by default and post only when their preference is on and the status is `granted`, at most once per day each.
7. Reflections survive export, merge import and backups, and Delete all data removes them.
8. No copy uses "missed", "failed", "broken" or "streak lost", an exclamation mark or an emoji (a copy test greps the Rust word modules and the feature).
9. Every new or changed component has its README, a component test and a specimen in both themes; `just contrast` passes; `./scripts/ci.sh` is green and every file stays at 500 lines or fewer.

## Non-goals
- Calendar blocks: SPEC 2.5's "plan next week's deep blocks", tomorrow's events and the realistic finish time (P3).
- Distraction data: "top distractions", bypass reasons, the focus vs distracting split (P5).
- Time per project or tag (follow-up). Leaderboards, sharing or any comparison with other people (never).
- A big streak number or a score of any kind (routines Decision 6).
- Editing or deleting one reflection from a list, and a reflection history page (follow-up).
- A choice of week start or of shutdown weekdays (follow-ups).

## Users & permissions
One local person, no accounts. The nudges use the existing notification permission and ask for nothing new. Reflections are personal writing: syncable like every other user entity (ADR-002), never sent anywhere until P10 sync, and covered by Delete all data.

## Tenancy & localization
- English copy, plain and kind. Rust words every date, duration, count and sentence (ADR-011); the webview assembles none.
- **Days** are local dates from `LocalDate::from_epoch_ms(ms, offset)` (`crates/core/src/routines/date.rs:92`), with the current offset, as the routines do. A focus session counts toward the local date it started on. Every session is bucketed with **today's** offset, so after a DST change or travel a session near midnight can move one day; this is accepted (the dot grid does the same), and a test pins it.
- **Across devices.** Two devices in different time zones can write the same `period` for different instants (a shutdown at 23:00 in one zone and 08:00 the next day in another). Accepted until P10 sync; the field merge below keeps both people's words.
- **Weeks start on Monday**, like the dot grid (routines Decision 5). Months are calendar months.
- **Shutdown day.** A shutdown started before 04:00 local belongs to the previous date, so a 00:40 shutdown closes the day that just ended and "tomorrow" is the date it now is. The shutdown day is frozen when the flow starts. Between 00:00 and 04:00 the overview's **Today** section shows that previous date ("Monday 16 November") and its status; from 04:00 it shows the new date. *Lean (owner may overrule on the canvas).* Everything else (stats, routines, the Board's Frog, the priorities' calendar week) keeps local midnight.
- **Planned week.** Monday to Thursday, the weekly review plans the current week and looks back on the week before. Friday to Sunday, it plans next week and looks back on the current week so far. *Lean (owner may overrule on the canvas).* It changes only at local midnight (Friday 00:00 and Monday 00:00), which the midnight `review-changed` covers.
- **Current week.** The calendar week that contains today, `week_start(today)`. The "· This week" marker and the shutdown's look at tomorrow use it, never the planned week, so priorities picked on Friday for next week read "· This week" only from that Monday.

## Domain model
New CONTEXT.md terms: Shutdown, Shutdown day, Reflection, Weekly review, Planned week, Current week, Look-back, Weekly priority, Focus time, Stats range, Buffer suggestion.

### `reflection` (syncable, `change_log` like `inbox_note`)
| Field | Type | Rules |
|---|---|---|
| `id` | TEXT PK | `shutdown:<YYYY-MM-DD>` or `week:<YYYY-MM-DD>` (the planned week's Monday), so two devices agree, as `habit_check` does |
| `kind` | TEXT | `shutdown` or `week` |
| `period` | TEXT | the shutdown day, or the planned week's Monday; `GLOB` CHECK like `habit_check.day` |
| `went_well`, `to_improve`, `outside_control` | TEXT NULL | shutdown only; trimmed, ≤ 1 000 characters, empty is NULL |
| `improvement` | TEXT NULL | week only; ≤ 200 |
| `notes` | TEXT NULL | week only; ≤ 2 000 |
| `priorities` | TEXT NOT NULL DEFAULT `'[]'` | week only; JSON array of 0–3 distinct task ids, in order |
| `completed_at` | INTEGER NULL | set when the flow reaches its last step; kept on later saves |
| `created_at`, `updated_at` | INTEGER | |
| `deleted_at` | INTEGER NULL | tombstone; nothing in this spec writes one, but merge import and sync honour it |

CHECKs tie the shutdown fields to `kind = 'shutdown'` and the week fields to `kind = 'week'`; `(kind, period)` is UNIQUE. `Entity` gains `Reflection`.

**Merge (portability now, sync in P10) is per field.** For each text field the later `updated_at` wins, except that a NULL never overwrites the other side's non-null text; `priorities` follows the later `updated_at`; `completed_at` takes the earliest non-null value. So two devices that each answered part of a shutdown keep both answers. This is the one table whose merge differs from the whole-row rule of ADR-018, and `crates/store/src/portability/merge` gets a `reflection` merger for it.

### Rules
- **A reflection is never scored.** It is shown back as the person's own words, with its day, and never counted ("5 reflections") or summarised.
- **A weekly priority** is a live task id. A priority whose task was removed is dropped from every view (the row keeps the id; Undo on the Board brings it back). A Done task reads "Done" with a check shape.
- **Focus time** is the sum of `focused_ms` over phase `focus` sessions, quick and ended early included: the same total as the deep-hours cap (CONTEXT, *Deep-hours cap*). The UI says "focus", never "deep hours", because Deep is also a task kind.
- **No denominators, no recaps.** Habit lines count days done only: "Done on 9 days this week", or "Done on 9 days since 3 November" for a habit created mid-range, which counts from its creation day. The dots show the schedule; no line says "of N". Routine lines count finished runs only (`outcome = finished`; `runs_on` also returns open and stopped runs). Removed (tombstoned) habits and routines don't appear in stats. A range with nothing in it says so plainly and no sentence names, counts or recaps a gap (design-system README, *One-time tips*: "A returning user is never welcomed back, counted or recapped").
- **Evening Shutdown routines stay plain routines.** Nothing reads `routine.template` to change behaviour (routines Decision 7, `docs/specs/2026-11-14-routines-and-habits.md:181`). The template data does not change, and a completed shutdown checks in no habit. See [FAQ](#how-do-evening-shutdown-routines-relate-to-the-guided-shutdown).

### Buffer suggestion
- **Units.** A task's estimate is a count of focus sessions (1–16, `docs/specs/2026-11-14-board.md:87`). Actual is also a count of focus sessions: the task's linked phase-`focus` sessions with at least 5 focused minutes. Five minutes is the shortest preset focus (`crates/core/src/timer/preset.rs:61`); shorter rows are the 2-minute quick start and stray starts, which the person never estimated. So the stats' actual can be lower than the card's "Actual 4 sessions", which counts every row; the line says "sessions of 5 minutes or more".
- **Eligible task:** Done, `done_at` in the last 56 local days, an estimate, and at least one counted session (from `focus_sessions` by `task_id`; `tasks::tracked`, `crates/store/src/repo/tasks.rs:299`, counts every row).
- **Threshold:** at least 10 eligible tasks, done in at least 3 different weeks. *Lean (owner may overrule on the canvas)*: ten tasks keep one long outlier from swinging a ratio of sums, and three weeks is "a few weeks of linked sessions" (#585). It cannot appear in the first week of use, which is honest.
- **Value:** `ratio = Σ actual sessions / Σ estimated sessions`; `suggested = clamp(round_to_5((ratio − 1) × 100), 25, 50)`, in integers in core, ties rounding up. 25–50 in steps of 5 are the Board's only allowed values (`docs/specs/2026-11-14-board.md:105`), so a suggestion can be lower than the current buffer (40% → 30%) but never below 25 or above 50.
- **Estimate line** (Stats and the look-back; amended 2026-11-16 at the plan, G1): Done tasks whose `done_at` falls in the range or the look-back's dates, with an estimate and at least one counted session. "6 finished tasks had an estimate: 14 sessions planned, 17 sessions of 5 minutes or more." No percentage, no judgement; `None` when no task qualifies.
- **Shown** only when `suggested` differs from the current buffer. *Not now* hides it for 28 days on this device (device-only `meta` key `hint.buffer_suggestion`, holding the time it was set aside); after that it can appear again with whatever value fits then.

## Interfaces

### Core (`crates/core/src/review/`, pure, time passed in)
- `week.rs`: `week_start(LocalDate) -> LocalDate` (Monday), `planned_week(today) -> LocalDate`, `look_back(planned) -> (LocalDate, LocalDate)`, `shutdown_day(epoch_ms, offset_min) -> LocalDate` (the 04:00 rule).
- `buckets.rs`: `focus_buckets(sessions: &[(LocalDate, u64)], range: StatsRange, today) -> Vec<Bucket { start: LocalDate, minutes: u32, sessions: u16, current: bool, future: bool }>`; `StatsRange` is `Week | Weeks8 | Months6`. Buckets are calendar-anchored: Week is 7 daily buckets, Monday to Sunday of the current week (days after today are `future` and empty); 8 weeks is the current partial week plus the 7 before it; 6 months is the current partial month plus the 5 before it.
- `habits.rs`: `days_done(created, from, to, done) -> (count, since: Option<LocalDate>)`; `since` is the creation day when it falls inside the range. The Week range's dots reuse `tracker::day_states` (`crates/core/src/routines/tracker.rs:36`).
- `buffer.rs`: `suggest(tasks: &[(LocalDate /* done */, u8 /* estimated sessions */, u32 /* counted sessions */)], today) -> Option<Suggestion { percent, tasks, estimated, actual }>`.

### Store
- `crates/store/src/repo/reflections.rs` (**Syncable**): `get(id)`, `upsert(&ReflectionWrite)` (logs only changed fields), `list(kind, from_period, to_period)`. No `rusqlite` type in the API; days are ISO strings (routines Decision 27).
- `routine_runs::finished_between(from_day, to_day)` (outcome `finished` only), beside `runs_on` (`crates/store/src/repo/routines/routine_runs.rs:155`, which returns every run of a day).
- `focus_sessions::counted_for_tasks(task_ids, min_focused_ms)` for the buffer's actual.
- Reused: `focus_sessions::started_between` (`crates/store/src/repo/focus_sessions.rs:113`), `habit_checks::checks_since` (`habit_checks.rs:105`), `tasks::list_done_since` and `tasks::tracked` (`tasks.rs:214`, `:299`), `tasks::last_backlog_rank` (`:252`).
- Moving the shutdown's chosen tasks: `tasks::move_to_backlog_end(ids) -> Vec<LaneBefore { id, lane, rank }>` and `tasks::restore_lanes(&[LaneBefore])`, each one transaction. The service keeps the `LaneBefore` list in memory behind an opaque token (ADR-013: product state lives in Rust; the webview holds only the token), one token at a time, dropped after 10 minutes or on the next move.

### Service and IPC (`crates/service/src/review/`, commands in `apps/orasenda/src-tauri/src/commands/review.rs`)
Types in `crates/service/src/api/review.rs` (`specta`, camelCase). Every `line`, `label` and `spoken` is Rust's sentence.
- `ReviewView { today: ShutdownStatus { day, dayLabel, done: bool, line /* "Ready when you are." | "Shutdown complete at 17:42." */ }, week: WeekStatus { weekStart, label /* "Planning the week of 23 November" */, planned: bool, line, priorities: Vec<PriorityView> }, primary: shutdown | weekly | none }`.
  - **Primary:** `weekly` from the weekly day (default Friday) until the end of the planned week's Monday, while the planned week has no completed review; else `shutdown` while the shutdown day's shutdown isn't complete; else `none`. *Lean (owner may overrule on the canvas).* With `review.weekly_day = mon`, the nudge comes on Monday and the review plans that same week (the planned-week rule), so the primary is weekly on Monday only.
- `PriorityView { taskId, title, done: bool, line /* "Fix sync bug · Doing" */ }`.
- `ShutdownView { day, dayLabel, unfinished: Vec<UnfinishedTask { id, title, laneLabel, active: bool }>, tomorrow: Vec<String>, answers: ShutdownAnswers { wentWell, toImprove, outsideControl: Option<String> }, completedLine: Option<String> }`. `tomorrow` lists the current week's priorities (`week_start(today)`), not the planned week's.
- `WeeklyView { weekStart, label, lookBack: LookBack, answers: WeeklyAnswers { improvement, notes: Option<String>, priorities: Vec<PriorityView> }, lastImprovement: Option<String>, completedLine: Option<String> }`.
  - `LookBack { heading /* "Monday 16 to Friday 20 November" */, focusLine, days: Vec<BarView>, goal: Option<GoalView>, tasks: Vec<BarView> /* top 5 by focus time */, tasksLine, routineLines, habitLines, estimateLine: Option<String>, reflections: Vec<ReflectionEcho { dayLabel, lines: Vec<String> }> }`.
- `StatsView { range: StatsRange, summary: Vec<String>, bars: Vec<BarView>, goal: Option<GoalView>, routines: Vec<String>, habits: Vec<HabitStat { name, line, dots: Option<Vec<HabitDotView>> }>, estimateLine: Option<String>, buffer: Option<BufferSuggestionView { percent, line, action /* "Use 40%" */ }>, empty: Option<String> }`.
  - **Goal by range:** on Week, `goal` is the week's total against the weekly goal, drawn as a goal `BarRow` above the day bars (a weekly goal has no per-day line); on 8 weeks, `goal` is the same weekly goal, drawn on `TrendBars` as a tick on each week's bar; on 6 months, `goal` is `None`. No goal setting means `None` everywhere.
  - **Dots:** `HabitStat.dots` is set on the Week range only (the existing 28-day `HabitDots`); 8 weeks and 6 months show the sentence only.
- `BarView { label /* "Tue" | "16 Nov" | "Oct" */, spoken /* "Tuesday 17 November, 2 hours 10 minutes" or "Saturday 21 November, nothing recorded" */, minutes, duration /* "2h 10m", empty when none */, current: bool, empty: bool /* no focus, or a future day */ }`; `GoalView { minutes, words /* "Goal 12h" */, line /* "You reached your 12h goal." or "9h 40m of a 12h goal." */ }`.
- Drafts: `ShutdownAnswers`, and `WeeklyDraft { improvement, notes, priorities: Vec<PriorityPick> }` with `PriorityPick = { task: id } | { newTitle: String }`. A draft is the full state: the service trims text, and an absent or empty field is written as NULL (an empty `priorities` is `[]`).
- **Periods.** Every write names its period, taken from the `*_get` view: `shutdown_complete(day, answers)`, `weekly_save(weekStart, draft)`, `weekly_complete(weekStart, draft)`. Rust accepts only the current period or the one just before it (today's or the previous shutdown day; the planned week or the week before it) and otherwise answers `Period` ("This review is for an earlier day. Open Review again to see today.").
- **Typed picks resolve once.** `weekly_save` and `weekly_complete` return the new `WeeklyView`, where every `{ newTitle }` is now a `PriorityView` with its new task id. The webview replaces its draft with that view, so going Back and saving again sends `{ task: id }` and never creates a second task.
- `ReviewError { NotFound, TooLong, Full /* a fourth priority */, Empty /* a blank new title */, Period, Store }`.
- Commands (main window only; `build.rs` `AppManifest`, `capabilities/main.json`): `review_get`, `review_stats(range)`, `shutdown_get`, `shutdown_move(ids) -> MoveUndo { token, line }`, `shutdown_move_undo(token)`, `shutdown_complete(day, answers) -> ShutdownView`, `weekly_get`, `weekly_save(weekStart, draft) -> WeeklyView`, `weekly_complete(weekStart, draft) -> WeeklyView`, `buffer_suggestion_dismiss()`.
- Event `review-changed` (`ReviewView`), emitted after every committed review write, outside the service lock, and at local midnight and at 04:00 local from the scheduler tick (built like the `SessionService::routines_day_view` method, `crates/service/src/routines/service.rs:74`). Midnight covers the planned-week change and the stats' new day; 04:00 covers the shutdown day. Board writes from this module also emit `board-changed`.
- `TaskView` gains `weekPriority: bool`: the task is a priority of the current week, `week_start(today)`, never `planned_week`. The TaskPicker marks it.

### Nudges (`crates/service/src/review/nudge.rs`)
Called from the scheduler tick beside `routines::nudge_due` (`crates/service/src/scheduler/tick.rs:44`) and built like it (`crates/service/src/routines/nudge.rs:46`): core `due_now` decides the 30-minute window (`crates/core/src/routines/due.rs:11`).
- Shutdown: Monday to Friday at `review.shutdown_time`, never once today's shutdown is complete. "Ready to close the day when you are." *Lean (owner may overrule on the canvas)*: weekdays only, because a work shutdown on a weekend nudges toward work.
- Weekly: on `review.weekly_day` at the shutdown time, never once the planned week is complete. "Your weekly review is ready when you are." If both are due in the same minute, only the weekly one posts and the shutdown counts as nudged.
- One review nudge per minute at most, and none in a minute when a routine's due nudge posted (an Evening Shutdown copy at 17:30, for example): the review nudge is then marked nudged for the day. This needs no link to the template (routines Decision 7); it only keeps two notifications from landing together.
- Same gate as the routines: the preference on **and** the status `granted`, read only when due; an in-memory `(kind, date)` set.

### Review page (`apps/orasenda/src/features/review/`)
- Replaces the `Placeholder` for `review` in `App.tsx`'s page switch; the page list and ⌘6 stay (`apps/orasenda/src/domain/navigation/PagesButton.tsx:8-16`).
- `useReview` holds `ReviewView` from `review_get` and `review-changed`; `useStats(range)` reads `review_stats` on mount, on a range change, and on `review-changed`, `routines-changed`, `board-changed` and `timer-milestone` with kind `ended` (so a session with no linked task, which emits no `board-changed`, still refreshes the bars). Writes are one in flight (conventions, *Newest wins*).
- **Overview** inside one `ScrollRegion` (pages never scroll; regions do): `PageFrame` "Review" with the one primary at the header's end (*Start shutdown* or *Start weekly review*, from `primary`; the other is a secondary button in its section). Section **Today** (day label, `line`, *Look again* after completion). Section **This week** (label, `line`, the priorities as a plain list with "Done" in words). Section **Stats**: a `Choice` (Week, 8 weeks, 6 months; default Week), the summary sentences, `TrendBars`, the routine and habit lines (with each habit's `HabitDots`, `columns="week"`, legend once, on the Week range only), the estimate line, and the buffer suggestion as an `InlineNotice` with *Use 40%* (secondary) and *Not now* (quiet).
- **Flows** replace the overview in place, like the run view (routines Decision 11), each in `FlowStep`. A step applies on *Continue*; *End* (Esc) keeps earlier steps and drops the current one; optional steps have a visible *Not now* (design-system README, *A step applies on Continue*). Focus moves to the step heading after each step; one polite live region says "Step 2 of 4, Tomorrow". The last step's *Back to Review* is focused. No single-key shortcuts: text fields need every key, and ⌘↩ is the fixed start-focus key.
- **Shutdown steps:** (1) *Unfinished tasks*: Today and Doing tasks, each with a toggle button *Back to Backlog* (`aria-pressed`, off by default; a task with a running session reads "In progress" and has no toggle). Continue moves the chosen ones in one write and shows a `Toast` "Moved 2 tasks to Backlog." with Undo and Dismiss. It is never auto-dismissed, because it has an action (`toast.md`): it goes on Undo, Dismiss, the next move or the end of the flow. The Rust token lasts 10 minutes; after that Undo says "That can't be undone now." With none: "Nothing unfinished." (2) *Tomorrow*, read-only: the date, routines scheduled with their times (those with no time follow, as "Name (any time)"), tasks due, the Today column's planned sessions with the buffer (Board wording), the week's priorities. (3) *Reflect*: three optional `TextArea`s, "What went well?", "What would you like to improve?", "What was outside your control?"; its Continue (or *Not now*, which sends the saved answers unchanged) calls `shutdown_complete`. (4) "Shutdown complete.", which only confirms.
- **Weekly steps:** (1) *Look back* (read-only; last week's improvement shown first when there is one: "Last week you chose: …"). ## Behavior & edge cases
- **Saving.** The shutdown writes once, on the Reflect step's Continue (step 1's moves are their own Board write). The weekly review writes on step 2 and step 3. *Look again* reopens a flow with saved answers; completing again keeps the first `completed_at`. Only the current step's unsaved typing is lost on reload, because the flow's position is webview state (Review is main-window only).
- **An old view.** A flow left open across midnight or 04:00 still sends the period it was opened with; the previous period is accepted, anything older gets `Period` and the plain line, and the overview reloads.
- **A shutdown across midnight** keeps the day frozen at its start. A shutdown on a day with no focus and no tasks is normal: every step still works.
- **Priorities.** A fourth pick is refused with `Full` (the UI never offers it). The same task twice is ignored. A new title is 1–200 characters (Board rules) and lands at the end of Backlog in the review's transaction, once (see *Typed picks resolve once*); Undo of the review is not offered, the task is removed on the Board like any other.
- **Moving tasks.** Undo sends the token and Rust restores each task's lane and rank; an expired or replaced token answers `NotFound` and the toast says "That can't be undone now." If the person edited a task in between, Undo still restores the lane; a removed task stays removed.
- **Empty states.** No data at all: "Nothing recorded yet. Stats appear after your first focus session." A range with no sessions: the bars show labels only and the summary says "Nothing recorded in this range yet." No reflections this week: the look-back leaves that part out.
- **Gaps.** A habit with check-ins until 1 October, then nothing until 3 November, reads on 8 weeks "Done on 14 days in these 8 weeks." The empty weeks show labels only; no line mentions them.
- **Copy.** Routine lines count finished runs only ("Wind-down: finished 6 times."), never runs against days; habit lines never say "of N". No "missed", "failed", "broken" or "streak lost"; no percentages except the buffer's own setting value; a reached goal is said plainly. `amber` and `critical` are not used anywhere here.
- **Store unavailable.** "Orasenda can't load your review right now." + Try again + "It's kept on this Mac." A failed save rolls back to Rust's last view and says "Orasenda can't save your reflection right now." (design-system README, *Copy patterns*).
- **Import and sync.** Reflections merge per field (see the table). A merged reflection for today reaches an open flow only when it is opened again; `review-changed` follows every import that touched the table.
- **Reduce motion.** Step changes cross-fade with `duration-calm`; under reduce-motion they change at once. Bars never animate.
- **Accessibility.** Hit targets `hit-min`; the TaskPicker and toggles follow their READMEs; each step's heading is the focus target; bars are read as a list of sentences.

## FAQ
### How do Evening Shutdown routines relate to the guided shutdown?
They are separate. A template is copied, never linked, so a copy is the person's routine and the app does not change what it does. A person can keep the routine as a checklist, remove it, or use the Review page instead. Changing the template's steps to point at Review would only affect new copies; that is left to the owner (Open questions).

### What do "move unfinished tasks" and "preview tomorrow" mean before P3?
There is no calendar yet, so nothing is scheduled. Moving means choosing which Today and Doing tasks go back to Backlog; the rest stay where they are. The preview reads what Orasenda already knows about tomorrow (routines, due dates, the Today column and its buffer, the week's priorities) and invents nothing. P3 adds events and blocks.

### Why one shared table and not `shutdown` and `weekly_review` tables?
Both are small, personal writing keyed by a period, and both travel through the same portability and sync rules. One repository, one merge rule, and SPEC 4's single `Review` entity.

### Why no "up from last week"?
A comparison turns a record into a score, and for a person returning after a gap it is a recap of the gap. Values and the person's own goal are enough.

### Why is the suggestion never applied by itself?
The buffer is the person's planning choice (Board Decision 7). History can inform it; only the person changes it (calm optional controls rule).
