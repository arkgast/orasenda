**Controller rulings on the plan's spec gaps (2026-11-16; the spec is amended in this PR).**
- **G1, the estimate line.** It covers Done tasks whose `done_at` falls in the range (Stats) or in the look-back's dates, with an estimate and at least one counted session (5 focused minutes or more). Rust words it as "6 finished tasks had an estimate: 14 sessions planned, 17 sessions of 5 minutes or more." There's no percentage and no judgement. It's `None` when no task qualifies.
- **G2, the review time.** `review.shutdown_time` ranges from 240 to 1439 (04:00–23:59), and a stored value outside that range reads as the default. So the shutdown day and the calendar date always agree when a nudge is due.
- **G3, the Undo toast.** It follows `toast.md` and is never auto-dismissed, because it has an action. It stays until the person chooses Undo or Dismiss, the next move happens, or the flow ends. The Rust token lasts 10 minutes, and after that Undo answers "That can't be undone now." The toast doesn't need to pause on hover or focus.
- **Minor.** `weekly_complete` emits `board-changed` when the planned week is the current week, so that `weekPriority` stays fresh (V19).
