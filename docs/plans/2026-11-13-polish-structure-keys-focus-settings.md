## Overview
H is cut into 22 tasks: 20 code tasks and two non-code ones, **HD** (the design gate) and **HS** (the spikes). The four waves are the owner's; the edges inside them follow file ownership.

### Waves
- **Wave 0, alone:** **H1a → H1b**, the frontend layers (ADR-021, order `surfaces → app → features → domain → shared`). These are moves only, with unchanged vitest and Playwright totals.
  - **H1a:** aliases (including `@styles/*`), the Biome scaffold, `just lint-layers`, `docs/conventions.md` and `shared/`. It also does the move-only splits of `test/fixtures.ts` and `dev/layout/scenarios.ts`, so every later task owns separate files.
  - **H1b:** `domain/`, the feature, surface and app moves, and the remaining rules.
- **Non-code, no dependencies:**
  - **HD**, the design gate: the controller runs Claude Design, the owner picks, and the spec is amended.
  - **HS**, the spikes: key route, Esc in native full screen, and the floater over our own full-screen Space. The controller runs them on `main` with a throwaway instrumented build and `osascript`.
- **Wave 1, keys and intent:**
  - **H2** removes the intent.
  - **H3a1** (bug a) runs beside H2 and merges after it.
  - **H3a2** (bugs b and c, `actions::run`, #439) follows.
  - **H3b1** builds the new Rust service files only, with no IPC change.
  - **H3b2** switches the IPC, menus, tray and import hooks (#438).
  - **H3c** does the webview.
- **Wave 2:**
  - **`packages/ui` first:** HU1 (`SettingsNav`) and HU2 (`DataTable`), whose scope the design pick sets; HU3 (`FullScreenFocus` and `duration-hint`); HU4 (any other variant the pick needs; may close).
  - **Full screen:** **H4a** runs beside **H3b2** once H3a2 lands. Then **H4b** (the swap in the app; also needs H3c and HU3), then **H4c** (the focus-window deletions).
  - **Settings:** **H5a**, then **H5b** ∥ **H5c**.
- **Wave 3:** **H6** ∥ **H7**. Both need only H5a (and HU2 for H7), so they may run beside H5b and H5c.

- **Registration files are append-only.** On a conflict the controller keeps both sides (`union.py`), then runs `cargo fmt` and `just bindings`, and fixes the exact capability lists. These files are:
  - `src-tauri/commands.list.rs`, `src-tauri/src/{commands/mod.rs,lib.rs,ipc.rs}` and `capabilities/*.json`;
  - `src-tauri/src/lib_tests/capabilities.rs`, `crates/service/src/api/{mod.rs,events.rs}` and `shared/ipc/bindings.ts`;

- **H2, H3b2 and H4c delete lines from these files.** For those three, the rule is **rebase and regenerate, never `union.py`**, because a union would bring the deleted lines back.
- **Every task that adds or removes a command** commits or deletes its `src-tauri/permissions/autogenerated/<command>.toml`. Those files are tracked.
